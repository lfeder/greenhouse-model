Revenue & investment

Base price/lb per variety computed once from invoices (sum $ / sum lb, Grade 1). Inflation slider scales prices up. Current revenue = current lb/day × days × price. Post-Exp = projected lb/day × days × price. Capex = new K/J/E acres × cost/ac (from expansion page sliders). Payback = capex / ΔRevenue.
