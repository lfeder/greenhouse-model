Marginal return per added acre

At each total-acre level, revenue = sum over months of min(1, ac/required) × target revenue. Early acres unlock easy months (summer); later acres only chip at winter peaks, so marginal revenue (and ROI) falls. Acres treated as a single pool (GHs rotate varieties). Required already grossed up for 5% slippage; revenue uses adjusted prices from the inflation slider. Cost per ac = cucumberCostPerAc.

Scenario comparison — E sized winter vs summer

Two scenarios: keep E at the winter (Jan) target year-round, or size E for the summer (Apr) target year-round. K and J targets are unchanged. Δ rev = (E summer − E winter) × 365 × price. Δ cost = extra E acres × cost/ac. Payback = Δ cost / Δ rev.
